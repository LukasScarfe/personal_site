[Oxalis triangularis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxalis_triangularis), also called the purple shamrock, grows from little bulb-like rhizomes and has deep purple, triangle-shaped leaves. The leaves fold down at night and open back up in the morning. It also goes dormant every so often: the leaves die back and the pot looks empty for a few weeks, then new growth comes back up from the rhizomes.
