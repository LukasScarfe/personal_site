[Oxalis triangularis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxalis_triangularis), grows from little potato rhizomes deep in the soil and has deep purple, triangle-shaped leaves. The leaves fold down at night and open back up in the morning thanks to lighting. It also goes dormant every so often when not watered.
